**Status:** Build · **Year:** Dec 2025 – Jan 2026 · **Event:** Microsoft Imagine Cup 2026 MVP Round

Amplify is a question-driven collective learning platform built for the Microsoft Imagine Cup
2026 MVP Round. It centers on a graph-based Agentic QA system that traces logic and verifies
facts against lecture materials instead of generating unverified answers, and turns student
interactions into real-time comprehension analytics for instructors.

Accessibility was a core design goal: Amplify integrates Azure Immersive Reader and interaction
algorithms designed with students with disabilities in mind, reducing cognitive load for
screen-reader users and helping visually impaired students formulate complex queries step by step.
