I am MinJae Choi, an undergraduate student at [KAIST](https://kaist.ac.kr) studying computer science, electrical engineering, and industrial design. I work around foundation model systems, quantization, graph algorithms, and product-minded engineering.

I am an undergraduate researcher at the [Flexible Machine Learning Algorithm Lab (FlexML)](https://flexml.kaist.ac.kr/), advised by Prof. Insu Han, where I work on near-1-bit quantization for trillion-scale foundation models. I am also active in [Include](https://kaist-include.io/), KAIST's AI club, where I have organized and taught seminars on generative models, graph AI, and computer vision.

Outside of research, I compete in AI/ML competitions (LG AI Research K-EXAONE compression competition, NVIDIA Nemotron reasoning challenge, Microsoft Imagine Cup) and design algorithms for combinatorial and graph problems (PACE Challenge, CJ Logistics palletizing). I also bring an Industrial Design background to my engineering work — see the [design portfolio](/design.html) for that side of things.

A public [wiki](/wiki.html) of my course notes from KAIST (CS / EE / ID) is also available, along with a small [competition-tracking dashboard](/falcon.html) I built for fun.
